The Person Behind the Work

About

Photo

Sandy Yoo

I'm an advertising creative with a mind for strategy and an eye for what makes people feel something. My work is built on the belief that the best campaigns don't just sell — they shift perspective.

Trained at the Academy of Art University with an MFA in Advertising, I've built campaigns for brands across fashion, beauty, automotive, food & beverage, and consumer goods. I work across disciplines — concept, copy, art direction — with a bias toward work that is spare, honest, and hard to ignore.

I'm drawn to briefs where the answer isn't obvious, brands with something real to say, and collaborators who'd rather make something uncomfortable than something safe.

MFA Advertising — Academy of Art University

Based in San Francisco, CA
